bx_BAT EQ - prototype (macOS, Apple Silicon + Intel)

Install
1. Right-click install.command and choose Open (macOS asks once because the file is unsigned).
   It copies the VST3 and AU into your user plug-in folders and clears the download quarantine.
2. In Ableton: Settings > Plug-ins > make sure "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" is on, then Rescan.
3. Find "bx_BAT EQ" under Plug-ins > Brainworx Prototype and drop it on your master or a bus.

Use
1. Pick an engineer mode on the right: Neutral, Clearmountain, Hardwell or Brainworx.
2. Press BAT and play the loudest part of the track. BAT needs 8 seconds of audio and pauses
   while the track is silent. Click BAT again to cancel.
3. BAT loads its suggestion into slot C and shows what it heard. Your own setting stays in A.
   Knobs that BAT moved glow blue. With Level match on, C is set to the same loudness as A,
   so you compare tone, not volume.
4. Press Hear A / Hear C (or the A and C buttons at the top) to compare.
   Switch modes to hear another suggestion for the same audio.
5. Keep it, tweak it, or Dismiss it to go back to A.

The graph shows what BAT heard (grey), the mode's target (dashed), the EQ (blue)
and the result (white).

Notes
- The EQ is a clean digital 5-band EQ laid out like the AMEK EQ 250. It is not the AMEK model.
- The engineer modes use placeholder target curves. They are not the engineers' own settings.
- This is an unsigned prototype build for internal testing.
